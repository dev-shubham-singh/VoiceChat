2. Privacy Policy

  • Calls. Voice calls go directly between the two users' devices (peer to peer) where possible, or through a relay server that cannot listen to them. We do not record or store calls.
  • Text chat. Messages pass through our server to reach the other person and are not stored.
  • Accounts. If you create an account we store your username, a scrambled (hashed) form of your password, your gender and your friends list. We never store the password itself. Guests have no account and nothing is kept after they leave.
  • What we store. When you create an ID we log the time, your IP address, a short ID reference and the Terms version you accepted, as proof of consent. When a user is reported we log the time, the reason and the IP addresses involved. Your nickname and gender are kept on your own device so the form is pre-filled next time.
  • Other users can see your IP address. In a peer-to-peer call, the other person's device connects to yours, so a technical user could find your IP address.
  • Why we store it. To prove consent, to block abusive users, and to answer lawful requests from authorities.
  • How long. [e.g. 180 days], then it is deleted unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Your rights. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your data, and may withdraw consent, by writing to [email].
  • Sharing. We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that run the site, and with authorities when the law requires it.

3. Community Rules

Break these and you will be blocked. Serious cases are reported to the authorities.

  • No one under 18. If the person you are talking to seems to be a minor, end the call and report it.
  • No sexual content involving minors, in any form. This is reported to law enforcement.
  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, or sexual remarks that the other person has not welcomed.
  • No asking for or sharing of private information such as addresses, phone numbers, bank details, passwords or OTPs.
  • No scams, advertising, or asking for money.
  • No recording or sharing a call or chat without consent.
  • No bots, automated calls, or attempts to break or overload the service.

Use the Report button during a call to flag anyone who breaks these rules.
